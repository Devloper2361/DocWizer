# Purpose

The DocWizer logo should instantly communicate professionalism, trust, intelligence, and simplicity.

It should remain recognizable at every size, from a Play Store icon to a website favicon.

---

# Logo Personality

The logo should feel:

* Modern
* Professional
* Intelligent
* Clean
* Premium
* Timeless

It must never feel childish, overly decorative, or cartoon-like.

---

# Logo Concept

The logo should combine two ideas:

1. A document or sheet of paper.
2. Intelligence or guidance represented by a subtle sparkle.

The sparkle represents smart document management rather than magic.

---

# Style

* Flat design
* Material Design inspired
* Minimal geometry
* Rounded corners
* Clean proportions
* No heavy gradients
* No complex shadows
* No unnecessary details

---

# Scalability

The logo must remain clear at:

* 24 × 24 px
* 48 × 48 px
* 128 × 128 px
* 512 × 512 px

# Usage Rules

Never stretch the logo.

Never rotate the logo.

Never add effects.

Never change proportions.

Never place it on visually noisy backgrounds.

Always preserve clear spacing around the logo.

---

# Goal

The logo should become instantly recognizable as the symbol of fast, reliable, privacy-first document management.
