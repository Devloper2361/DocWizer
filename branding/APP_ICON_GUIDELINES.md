# APP_ICON_GUIDELINES.md

Project: DocWizer

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

---

# Objective

The application icon should be recognizable within one second while remaining clean and elegant.

---

# Design Goals

The icon should communicate:

* Documents
* Simplicity
* Trust
* Intelligence
* Professional quality

---

# Visual Direction

Use a simple folded document shape.

Include a subtle sparkle integrated into the design.

Avoid excessive decorative elements.

---

# Material 3 Principles

The icon should follow Material Design principles while maintaining its own identity.

Rounded geometry.

Balanced spacing.

Minimal details.

---

# Avoid

* Clipart
* Cartoon graphics
* Wizard hats
* Magic wands
* Overly detailed illustrations
* Heavy gradients
* 3D effects

---

# App Store Requirements

The icon should remain recognizable at all supported Android launcher sizes.

It should be distinctive enough to stand out without relying on bright colors or excessive visual effects.

---

# Success Criteria

Users should recognize the DocWizer icon quickly and associate it with reliability and premium quality.
